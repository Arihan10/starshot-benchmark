Every object you see is real.

SceneBench is a public evaluation from Starshot Labs, the research lab behind multi-object scene diffusion — a generative model that diffuses an entire 3D region into discrete, game-ready objects instead of a single fused mesh.

A language model reads the prompt, plans the scene, and orchestrates many diffusion canvases into one cohesive world. SceneBench puts those plans head-to-head: two models, one prompt, blind. Your votes rank the frontier.
