pano walkthrough

360° tour viewer ← studio
capturing a tour:
  1. in the main viewer, place the camera and hit 📷 capture 360 (repeat from several spots)
  2. save tour downloads tour.json, one JPEG per pano, and a low-poly proxy.glb
  3. drop all of those files here

with proxy.glb → walkthrough: the panos are projected onto the low-poly scene, so travelling between capture points interpolates with real parallax.
without it → 360 spheres you click between.

drag to look · scroll to zoom · click a floor disc to travel
