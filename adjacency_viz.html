Axes: +X right, +Y up, +Z toward you. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Pairwise visibility connects surface points of each zone pair and keeps the pair if any segment is unobstructed (catches far / small zones the raycast misses; toggle "show blocked connections" to see why a zone is dropped). Raycast shoots a sphere of rays from the box surface (K origins × M dirs) and keeps each ray's first hit.
